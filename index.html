About Jeff

Protecting Assets
at National Scale

"I have been in the business for decades and, until Hustad, had yet to encounter a roofing company that's truly the total package."

Founded in Madison, Wisconsin in 1973, HUSTAD Companies has evolved from a regional contractor into a nationally licensed and bonded partner operating across 46 states. Jeff Knapp leads national business development, forging MSA partnerships with property owners, federal agencies, DOD contractors, healthcare systems, and institutional portfolios.

Under Jeff's leadership, HUSTAD delivers AI-driven storm tracking, drone inspection capabilities, and executive-ready CapEx reporting — giving ownership groups the intelligence they need to protect NOI before damage occurs.
